Projects on a phone — three propositions

Each phone runs the same mockup at 390 × 844 on sample data. Search, chips, the ⚙ sheet, folders and the + button work; opening a project shows a toast. Open any one alone with index.html#a / #b / #c — on your phone too, same URL.

nowCurrent page

  • Title, subtitle, a wide "New project" button, search, two selects and wrapping chips take the top 40 % before the first project.
  • The list scrolls inside a 72 vh box inside a page that also scrolls.
  • Folders are drag-and-drop only; the "New folder" control is 28 px tall.

AOne list, folders fold

  • Same structure as the desktop tree, resized for a thumb: 56 px rows, 44 px thumbnails, folders fold with a chevron, ⋯ on a folder opens a sheet (rename, new inside, move, delete).
  • Search + one filter button; chips on a single scrolling rail.
  • Deep folders indent 16 px a level — fine for two levels, tight beyond.

BFolder browser (drill in)

  • One folder at a time, like the Files app: subfolders first, then the projects here, big 72 px rows with client and file on their own lines. Back arrow + breadcrumb in the title.
  • Search ignores the folder and shows flat results with their path.
  • + creates where you are (the menu says so). More taps to see everything.

CVisual grid

  • Two-column cards led by the part thumbnail; code, production flame and recommended file sit on the picture; name, client and printed count below.
  • Folders are tiles on a rail at the top (a 2 × 2 mosaic of their parts); tapping one scopes the grid, a chip undoes it.
  • Best for recognising a part by eye; long names clamp to two lines, fewer rows per screen.
In every proposition
  • The + button from the Aito board, fixed bottom-right in the safe area: + turns into × and opens a two-row menu, New project / New folder. The header button goes away on phones.
  • The page is exactly the screen under the top bar; the header stays, only the list scrolls (same recipe as the Aito phone board — no nested 72 vh box).
  • Status and sort move behind one button (a dot shows when they are not the defaults) into a bottom sheet; the three quick chips and the materials stay visible on a rail.
  • Touch targets 44 px or more everywhere; no drag-and-drop on phones — folders are managed from their ⋯ sheet, projects from the project page (Move to folder…).
  • Tap a row = open the project page, as today below lg.
