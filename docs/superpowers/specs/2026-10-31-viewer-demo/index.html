Propositions on top of option A — each one works here unless marked mock. Tell me which to keep.
0View cube (top-left), as in a CAD: click a face, an edge or a corner to turn the part to that view (26 positions), drag the cube to orbit, the small house returns to the isometric view. It always shows where you are looking from. 1Perspective / Orthographic toggle in the toolbar (key P). Ortho for checking proportions and alignment, perspective for looking at the part. 2Section plane: a slider cuts the part along Z (or X/Y) to check inner geometry, wall thickness and holes without X-ray. 3Dimensions overlay: the bounding box with X·Y·Z lengths on the part, plus the hovered object's own size. 4Measure: click two points on the part, read the distance. (mock here: a placed measurement) 5Isolate an object: click it in the Part card (or double-click it) to ghost the others and fit the view on it. Click again to show all. 6Printer bed drawn at the real size of the profile's printer (H2S 350×320 here, A1 mini 180×180…) with the printable area, so an out-of-bed object is obvious. 7Toolpath layer range: top and bottom handles, and the estimated print time at the chosen layer; feature toggles in the legend hide supports, infill or walls to inspect the rest. 8Compare with another revision: the picked revision as a translucent ghost over the current one, to see what moved. mock 9Turntable (auto-rotate) and a clean view that hides every control — for showing a client, or a timelapse-style check. 10Snapshot: save the current view as a PNG, and Use as thumbnail for the file card (the app already does this for STEP files). 11Pins: click to pin a note on the part ("chamfer here", "support this edge") saved with the revision, shown to whoever opens it next. mock 12Copy link to this exact view (file, tab, plate) for a colleague. mock 13Checks card: what the slicer data tells us — objects outside the bed, supports used, a filament slot not loaded, outdated against a newer revision. mock
