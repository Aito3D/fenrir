Where the numbers come from
  • Paid is the sum, over the order's invoices, of invoice total minus invoice balance. Zoho already sends this with each order, so there is no extra call.
  • Percentage is paid divided by the order total, rounded down, so 100% only ever means fully paid.
  • The bar has up to three parts: paid, invoiced and awaiting payment (red once the due date has passed), and the empty track for what is not invoiced yet.
  • Zoho's own "partially paid" flag only says "partially invoiced" in this shop's data: every invoice on today's open orders is paid. The amounts are more honest than the flag.
  • Contact is the contact person Zoho stores on the order (name, email, phone, mobile). It is already in the synced data. About a third of today's open orders have no phone and a few no email, so the block says so instead of hiding.
  • Paid in advance (hatched green, Propositions on): in the real data, 20 of 25 open orders that Zoho calls "unpaid" belong to a customer holding unused credit, usually the exact order total or a 50 to 70% deposit. The money is received but waits for the invoice. Zoho stores that credit per customer, not per order, so with several open orders it is handed out oldest order first.
  • Finished orders: Zoho's order list is already copied locally for every order, so the list costs nothing. Lines, invoices and contact of a finished order are fetched from Zoho the first time someone opens it, then kept. Until then the row only knows Zoho's paid, partly paid or unpaid flag, so it shows no percentage.
  • Shopify number or quote number, pick-up and payment terms are on the order in Zoho when someone filled them in. Delivery address never is.
