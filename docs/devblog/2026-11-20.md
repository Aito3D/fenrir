# Fenrir devblog — 20 November 2026

Image `ghcr.io/aito3d/fenrir:sha-ce593fa` (also `:latest`). Previous image: `sha-a00e755`, published earlier the same day.

This release is mostly about the revenue page (Chiffre d'affaires), plus a round of fixes on the order board.

## Revenue page

### New

- **Gross margin breakdown.** Each period now shows what its costs are made of: Zoho item costs, then for services the filament, energy, printer wear and labour. The breakdown is in the margin tooltip and in the opened period row of the table.
- **Filament at purchase price.** A print's filament is costed at the calculator's purchase price for its material, without margin. If a material has several price sheets, the cheapest is used; the recorded cost is used only when the grams are unknown.
- **Month picker.** The date range popover has a month grid. Picking a month selects the whole month (up to today for the current one).
- **Cash and order book panel in Évolution.** It shows this month's collected versus invoiced amounts (TTC), what is owed and what is overdue, the top overdue invoices (for users allowed to see customers), and the order book: accepted quotes not yet invoiced, per board column with deposits already collected, and sent quotes awaiting an answer. These are whole-business figures, so the panel appears only on the unfiltered page.
- **Collected versus invoiced per month, and outstanding receivables.** Amounts are cut to the 3D share of each invoice, so a customer who also buys non-3D goods does not inflate the figures.
- **New versus returning customers.** The Clients view splits each period's sales between first-time buyers, earlier buyers and unidentified sales (no customer, or a walk-in).
- **Services turnover per printer hour.** Shown in the detail table, in a hero tile and in the CSV export.
- **Month check against Zoho.** Each closed month is compared with Zoho's own monthly total. A month that does not match gets a reload button.

### More reliable figures

- **Past margins no longer move.** Each sales line keeps the item's unit cost and tax rate from the day it was fetched, so a later price change does not rewrite past margins or TTC amounts. Days fetched before this release still use the item's current values.
- **Partly costed items.** An item with a cost on only some of its lines now counts only those lines as costed.

### Sync

- **One bad day no longer blocks the queue.** A day that keeps failing waits out its own back-off while the other days sync.
- **A Zoho outage no longer marks days as failed.** Only an error specific to a day marks it. Token, network and Zoho-wide errors mark nothing.
- **Refused steps wait.** A contact or month check that Zoho refuses waits 6 hours instead of failing every batch.
- **Rate limits are honoured** in the cash step (Zoho's Retry-After).
- **Slow reads are logged.** A read of the revenue data that takes over 200 ms writes a warning to the log.

### Display

- Cash and order book tiles wrap on a phone instead of cutting amounts.
- The per-hour tile has its own name, hero amounts are shown whole, and the month reload is an icon.
- Amber text is readable on the light theme, hero tiles fit the available width, and tooltips open on keyboard focus.

## Order board

Six fixes change what an operator sees:

- **New-order cart.** Removing a line no longer erases an item added or scanned during the 200 ms removal animation.
- **Card terminal, failed status check.** The screen keeps "charge in progress" and keeps polling, instead of dropping the charge from view.
- **Card terminal, failed lookup.** Opening the terminal dialog when the charge lookup fails shows the live charge's waiting screen, or an error message. It no longer offers a fresh charge form while a charge is still running.
- **Saved filters, early pick.** Picking a filter before the saved board settings have loaded no longer wipes the other saved settings. If the settings failed to load, picks apply on screen but are not saved.
- **Saved filters, order.** Filter changes reach the server in the order they were picked, so a quick double change no longer saves the older choice.
- **Batch prep sheets.** An order that left the board while the batch was printing is skipped and the rest print. Before, the whole batch failed after some orders had already been marked as printed.

Under the hood:

- Prep-sheet PDFs are rendered off the server's main loop, so printing a large batch no longer freezes the app for everyone else.
- About 65 new tests cover the order routes and screens: payment refusals, the barcode scan hook, the new-order client picker, SMS sending, PDF downloads and every order event kind.

## Upgrading

Pull `ghcr.io/aito3d/fenrir:latest` and restart the container. The database updates itself at startup (two new columns on the revenue day lines, and new revenue tables); there is no manual step.
